These are interactive question counts, not counts of printed numbered exercises.
A multi-blank dialogue may be one question; a table may supply many. Repeated
workbook items are represented once in the app, with additional source references.
Source IDs with suffixes identify adapted subgroups, not extra printed exercises.
Level assignments are editorial. Some picture and matching tasks test the same
grammar using a supplied noun or context instead of requiring the original image.
